`Alert::area` returns the bar an alert takes on the screen at an `AlertSide`: `Top`, `Middle` or `Bottom`, at the full width and the alert's height. Draw the alert after the content, and it covers it without taking the content's bold or italic. A loud error or warning puts its tape in the top edge of the card, with a blank row under it and under the title block. A loud error shows an inverted `!`, and the loud action is a filled button in the text color. The gallery LoudAlert page has a position control, so the bar spans the whole terminal at the top, middle or bottom.
