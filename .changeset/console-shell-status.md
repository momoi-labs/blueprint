ConsoleShell takes an optional `status` node and shows it in the header, directly left of the account menu. Kiso adds `.app-shell-status` for that slot: it keeps the status and the account trigger at full size, and on phones the breadcrumb clips instead of running under the status.

The collapsed sidebar rail in the inset app shell is now narrower by the outer gap, so the rail and the gap together match the non-inset rail. Its icons, rail mark and toggle sit centred between the screen edge and the main panel, instead of leaving a wider margin on the panel side.
