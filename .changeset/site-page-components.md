Add the Site page pattern and its components: SiteSection (with SiteSectionHead, SiteSectionBody and SiteSectionFoot), SiteHeader, SiteHeading, SiteFooter, FeatureGrid, FeatureList, WindowFrame and PerspectiveStack. Sections take a default, inverse or accent tone, a glow, and a stack, center, split, row or grid layout; SiteHeader sticks, follows the band under it and folds into a menu or hides its links below 760px. WindowFrame gains terminal marks.

Add three token axes beside the accent: `data-neutral="zinc"` for a cool, near-black canvas, `data-primary="contrast"` for a main action in the text color, and `data-typeface="dm-sans"` with the new `--type-weight-heading`. The AA gate checks every combination.

CodeBlock gains `prompt`, a marker Copy leaves out, and `wrap`, with Copy as an icon beside the code. Chart gains `layout="spark"`, `headline` and `defaultPinned`. Tabs gains `variant="pills"` and a `keyHint` on each trigger.
