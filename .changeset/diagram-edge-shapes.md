Diagram edges take a shape and a head. `edges` on Diagram, or `shape` on a
DiagramEdge, draws them `orthogonal` (default), as a `curve`, `straight`, or
as a hand-drawn `sketch` that pairs with the Manga and Brush frames. `arrow`
picks the head: `triangle` (default), `chevron`, `stealth`, `dot`, `sketch`
or `none`. Routes, trunks and dots do not change, and the default drawing is
the same as before.
