`GridPane` and `DashboardPanel` take `frame="none"`. The pane draws no Card, so a pane that holds one `TableFrame` shows one frame under its title and actions. The reader moves and resizes it like any other pane. CSS consumers set `data-frame="none"` on `.grid-pane`.
